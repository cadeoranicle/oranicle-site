Canonical Interpretation

The viewer supports multiple regional canonicals. A provider can be projected relative to the selected regional manifold and viewed in either PCA 3D or UMAP 3D space.

Controls

  • Region — choose the canonical region
  • Geometry Mode — switch between UMAP 3D and PCA 3D
  • NPI — locate a specific provider in the selected region

View Modes

  • PCA 3D — formal canonical axis view
  • UMAP 3D — manifold topology view

What this means

Regional canonical manifolds are generated offline from provider-month behavioral data and then served as deterministic geometry layers. Provider lookup highlights a selected NPI relative to the active region’s archetypes and manifold structure.
